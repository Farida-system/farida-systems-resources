Campaign key visual for an iced latte served in a tall clear glass. DIRECTION: PREMIUM EVENING.

SUBJECT: a tall clear glass of iced latte with distinct layered coffee and milk, large clear ice cubes, light condensation on the glass. No branding, no printed text, no logo, no label anywhere.
AUDIENCE AND USE: evening speciality-coffee campaign for an independent cafe, positioned as a crafted treat rather than a fast drink.
COMPOSITION: glass placed in the lower right third on a dark walnut wood counter. The entire upper 40 percent of the frame is empty background reserved for Arabic headline copy.
CAMERA: 50mm, slightly above eye level, shallow depth of field.
LIGHTING: single warm key light from the back left creating a soft rim on the glass edge and a long shadow forward, deep falloff into the background.
TEXTURE: believable condensation droplets, real ice with internal fractures, natural milk swirl, visible wood grain. Coffee must look like real liquid, not plastic or resin.
COLOR DIRECTION: deep espresso brown, near-black background, warm caramel highlights, soft cream.
REALISM: photorealistic food photography.
MUST NOT APPEAR: text, lettering, logos, watermarks, hands, straws, scattered coffee beans, flowers, multiple glasses, studio reflections.
OUTPUT: vertical 4:5 with clean empty text-safe area at the top.
