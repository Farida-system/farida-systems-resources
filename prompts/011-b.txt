Campaign key visual for an iced latte served in a tall clear glass. DIRECTION: FRESH MORNING.

SUBJECT: a tall clear glass of iced latte with distinct layered coffee and milk, large clear ice cubes, light condensation on the glass. No branding, no printed text, no logo, no label anywhere.
AUDIENCE AND USE: morning campaign for an independent cafe, positioned as a bright everyday start to the day.
COMPOSITION: glass placed in the lower left third on a pale oak table beside a window. The entire upper 40 percent of the frame is bright empty background reserved for Arabic headline copy.
CAMERA: 50mm, eye level, moderate depth of field.
LIGHTING: soft diffused daylight from the right, gentle natural shadows, airy and open, no dramatic contrast.
TEXTURE: believable condensation droplets, real ice, natural milk swirl, fine matte wood grain. Coffee must look like real liquid, not plastic.
COLOR DIRECTION: warm white, pale oak, soft mint green accent, light caramel.
REALISM: photorealistic food photography.
MUST NOT APPEAR: text, lettering, logos, watermarks, hands, straws, scattered coffee beans, flowers, multiple glasses, studio reflections.
OUTPUT: vertical 4:5 with clean empty text-safe area at the top.
