Campaign key visual for an iced latte served in a tall clear glass. DIRECTION: DELIVERY OFFER.

SUBJECT: a tall clear glass of iced latte with layered coffee and milk and clear ice, viewed from directly above as a flat lay on a plain surface. No branding, no printed text, no logo, no label anywhere.
AUDIENCE AND USE: delivery promotion for an independent cafe, designed so pricing and offer copy can be added later.
COMPOSITION: top-down flat lay, glass positioned in the lower right quadrant. The left half and the top third of the frame are completely empty flat surface reserved for Arabic offer copy and a price block.
CAMERA: overhead 90 degree flat lay, everything in sharp focus.
LIGHTING: even soft light, minimal shadow, clean and legible, suitable for overlaying text.
TEXTURE: believable condensation, real ice, natural milk swirl. Coffee must look like real liquid, not plastic.
COLOR DIRECTION: flat warm sand background, deep coffee brown, cream, single muted terracotta accent.
REALISM: photorealistic food photography.
MUST NOT APPEAR: text, lettering, logos, watermarks, hands, straws, cutlery, napkins, scattered beans, multiple glasses.
OUTPUT: vertical 4:5 flat lay with large clean empty area for text on the left and top.
